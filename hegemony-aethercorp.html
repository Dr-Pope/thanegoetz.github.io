The Seven / Entertainment & luxury

AetherCorp

“Elevate Your Existence”

AetherCorp sells entertainment, luxury, culture, and experiences. Its products include real beauty and pleasure, but also the aspiration to belong to a world most people can only watch.

What it controls

Resorts, tourism, media, fashion, prestige markets, experience design, and cultural influence.

Life in its orbit

Artists and designers may find creative freedom while performers and service workers face exclusivity clauses, housing dependency, and reputation pressure. Status can become a product that is never quite paid for.

Places & presence

Elysium Station is its orbital headquarters. Resort habitats, studios, cultural enclaves, and prestige districts form its wider presence.

Follow the connections

Helix supplies enhancement for premium experiences. Nexus provides the information infrastructure on which media and audience access depend.
