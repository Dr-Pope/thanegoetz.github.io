Seven powers.
One connected civilization.

Welcome to Corporate Hegemony: a science-fiction universe where corporations are governments, citizenship has consequences, and the systems that sustain humanity also shape its freedom.

Everything has a price, but some things remain priceless.

Start with an industry you recognize. Follow its connections to discover who builds the ships, keeps a world habitable, moves its people, or finances its future.

The Seven / Corporate Registry

01 / Genetics & enhancement

Helix Industries

Medicine, cloning, augmentation, and genetic engineering make Helix indispensable to human life. Its influence reaches from hospitals and pharmaceutical production to the technologies used to reshape the body.

04 / Energy & terraforming

Helios Syndicate

Helios supplies the energy systems, fuel infrastructure, and environmental engineering that make large-scale settlement possible. Its greatest achievement is also the basis of its leverage: entire communities depend on systems it built.

05 / Entertainment & luxury

AetherCorp

AetherCorp sells entertainment, luxury, culture, and experiences. Its products include real beauty and pleasure, but also the aspiration to belong to a world most people can only watch.

06 / Finance & institutional risk

Meridian Financial Authority

Meridian sits beneath the banks and markets that finance interstellar civilization. Its power lies in clearing, reserves, insurance, development finance, and the ability to keep capital moving across human space.

07 / Transport & logistics

Wayline Interstellar

Developing entry

Wayline coordinates the journey between worlds: cargo collection, warehousing, port handling, connections, fleets, and delivery. Its advantage is the network joining these services together.

Power through dependence

No single corporation supplies everything civilization needs. Medicine needs manufacturing; industry needs energy; settlements need transport, information, and finance. Their dependence on one another keeps the balance uneasy.

These introductory dossiers explore the setting without revealing private campaign files or book plot twists.

An evolving universe: Wayline’s name and institutional details remain provisional in the worldbuilding notes. This edition keeps those details visibly separate from the established roster of seven.
