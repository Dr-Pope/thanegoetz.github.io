Helix Industries

“Engineering the Future of Humanity”

Medicine, cloning, augmentation, and genetic engineering make Helix indispensable to human life. Its influence reaches from hospitals and pharmaceutical production to the technologies used to reshape the body.

What it controls

Therapies, cloned organs, genetic modification, proprietary biological systems, and premium longevity treatments.

Life in its orbit

Enhancement offers opportunity, but licenses, treatment costs, and access divide society. Helix can hold patents over a therapy or its commercial production; constitutional protections prohibit ownership of the modified person.

Places & presence

Echelon Prime is its operational capital. The Spire of Ascension is its primary biotechnology and state complex; the Boston Research Arc anchors its principal Earth executive presence.
