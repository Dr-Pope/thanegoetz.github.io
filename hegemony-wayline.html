Wayline Interstellar

Developing entry · working name and details

“Keeping Worlds Connected.”

Wayline coordinates the journey between worlds: cargo collection, warehousing, port handling, connections, fleets, and delivery. Its advantage is the network joining these services together.

What it controls

Scheduled freight capacity, passenger services, ports and terminals, warehousing, fleet coordination, and physical courier carriage.

Life in its orbit

Port communities and crews live by shifts, safe handovers, maintenance, and the promise of an arrival. Reliable service and profitable service do not always point toward the same destination.

Places & presence

The current draft describes a Sol-rooted network with major Core hubs and smaller frontier feeder services. Its capital and named territorial holdings remain open.

Follow the connections

Nexus supplies information and navigation capabilities; Meridian finances and insures capacity. These relationships do not imply exclusive ownership of routes, carriers, or infrastructure.
