Helios Syndicate

“We Built Your World”

Helios supplies the energy systems, fuel infrastructure, and environmental engineering that make large-scale settlement possible. Its greatest achievement is also the basis of its leverage: entire communities depend on systems it built.

What it controls

Quantum Fuel infrastructure and certification, terraforming technology, energy networks, climate systems, and long-term infrastructure concessions.

Life in its orbit

Life on a terraformed world depends on maintenance as much as a breathable atmosphere. Service priorities, prices, and contracts become political questions when a colony wants greater independence.

Places & presence

Sol Station in Earth orbit is its historic center. Nova Terra is a mature terraforming showcase; New Eden is an agricultural success with growing local autonomy.

Slip Gates support safe and predictable travel. They are not portals, and Helios does not create or own the natural Slipstream channels.

Follow the connections

Kronos builds the industrial hardware; transport operators move the people, equipment, and supplies that keep settlements functioning.
