Kronos Industrial

“Building Tomorrow’s Universe”

Starships, habitats, industrial equipment, weapons, and household appliances all pass through the reach of Kronos. It supplies much of the physical fabric of interstellar civilization.

What it controls

Heavy industry, construction, shipbuilding, robotics, consumer products, and security hardware.

Life in its orbit

Industrial work brings questions of safety, automation, housing dependency, relocation debt, and the power of productivity systems. Legal freedom and the practical ability to leave an employer can be very different things.

Places & presence

Forge Prime is the principal industrial heartland. Shipyards, factory worlds, mining operations, and worker cities extend its reach beyond any single planet.

Follow the connections

Industry needs energy and terraforming infrastructure; the populations building it need medicine and biological support.
