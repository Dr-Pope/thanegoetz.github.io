Meridian Financial Authority

“Building Tomorrow’s Security.”

Meridian sits beneath the banks and markets that finance interstellar civilization. Its power lies in clearing, reserves, insurance, development finance, and the ability to keep capital moving across human space.

What it controls

Interstellar clearing, institutional banking, colonial development finance, insurance, settlement ratings, reserves, and systemic risk.

Life in its orbit

A loan or insurance decision can determine whether a settlement grows. Stability Ratings influence investment and premiums; their effects make the boundary between describing risk and shaping a future politically contentious.

Places & presence

Its sovereign center is in the Earth–Luna economy. Concord Station occupies a maintained Earth–Moon L1 halo orbit within Meridian Cislunar Territory.

Meridian Financial Authority and Meridian Station are distinct entries in the setting. A shared name does not make the station its sovereign capital.

Follow the connections

Financing and insurance support transport capacity and planetary infrastructure, but capital cannot substitute for ships, energy, or skilled labor.
